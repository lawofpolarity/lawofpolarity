## Cross-repository findings

1. **Artifact multiplicity is not semantic multiplicity.** Different repositories may instantiate the same underlying object.
2. **Historical terminology can collide with canonical terminology.** Such collisions require explicit reconciliation rather than silent reuse.
3. **Executable code is evidence of implementation behavior, not evidence of the theory's external interpretation.**
4. **Generated/inferred content must remain distinguishable from retrieved evidence.**
5. **Many-to-one transformations require information-loss/recoverability accounting.**
6. **Unresolved obligations must survive migration/reformatting until actually resolved.**
7. **A projection cannot acquire greater authority than its source merely through presentation or deployment.**

These findings are candidates for formal constitutional treatment after the public-repository review; this file records them without declaring a constitutional amendment.
